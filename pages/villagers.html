About villagers

Villagers are the friendly characters who live on your island. Each one has a unique appearance, personality and way of interacting with the player, helping create the warm and welcoming atmosphere that defines Animal Crossing.

Personality types

Every villager belongs to one of several personality groups. These personalities influence their dialogue, hobbies and daily behaviour. For example, a Peppy villager is energetic and cheerful, while a Lazy villager is relaxed and often talks about food or naps.

How does a villager move to your island?

A villager can move to your island in several ways. The most common method is meeting them on a mystery island and inviting them to live with you. They may also appear at your campsite, where you can talk to them and convince them to stay. Finally, villagers can move in through the housing plots you place, which allows new residents to arrive automatically over time.
