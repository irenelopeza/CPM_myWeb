What are items?

In Animal Crossing, an item refers to any object that can be stored in the inventory, displayed in a house or placed around the island.

Crafting system

Many items are created through the crafting system. Each craftable object has an internal identifier, such as ftr_wooden_table_01, which the game uses to manage variations and recipes.

Crafting was introduced to give players more freedom and creativity in shaping their island and making it more themselves.
— Nintendo Developer Interview

Item categories

Items in the game are usually grouped into different categories depending on their purpose and use. Below is a simple list of common item types:

  • Furniture
  • Tools
  • Clothing
  • Materials
  • Bugs
  • Fishes
  • Sea creatures
  • Flowers
  • Fossils
