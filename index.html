About us

Animal Crossing InfoHub is a small fan project created to share clear and friendly information about the game. We focus on helping players explore characters, items and the charm of the series.

What is Animal Crossing?

Animal Crossing is known for its relaxing atmosphere and its strong focus on community, making it one of the most beloved life-simulation games. Players are encouraged to take things at their own pace, enjoy small daily moments, and build meaningful connections with their villagers. This gentle approach, combined with its charming visuals and warm tone, has helped the series become a comforting space for millions of players around the world.

Learn more on the official Nintendo website.

Our mission

Our mission is to provide a welcoming place where players can learn, explore and connect with Animal Crossing. We focus on offering useful information, easy-to-read guides and a calm environment that reflects the relaxing spirit of the games.
